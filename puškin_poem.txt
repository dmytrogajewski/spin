The Bronze Phalanx, a tale of glory,
Stands tall in memory's hallowed shore.
Each soldier's oath, a hymn to freedom,
In battle's dance, the soul is stirred.

The shield's iron, the spear's gleam,
A symbol of courage and claim.
Through storms and strife, the flag remains,
A beacon of hope in darkest chains.

The march of time, the weight of years,
Is etched in every scar and tear.
Yet in the heart of every man,
There burns a fire, never gone.

So let the world recount this tale,
Of valor, faith, and steadfast care.
The Bronze Phalanx, a legend born,
In every soul, it's ever won.
